Story Behind the Design

My work is rooted in soft emotions, self-growth, and quiet healing. Each illustration reflects warmth, reflection, and the journey of becoming your truest self. These pieces are created as gentle reminders that light always exists within everyday life.

Carry A Little Light Series

This series expresses the idea that everyone carries an inner light. Even in quiet moments, that light continues to exist, gently guiding and supporting you. Each character is created as a symbolic blessing of comfort, love, and healing energy.

Guardian Angel

A symbol of protection and inner strength. It reflects the unseen support around you and the courage already within you.

Love Angel

A reminder that love begins within yourself. When you care for yourself gently, that warmth flows outward naturally.

Healing Angel

A gentle encouragement toward renewal and self-trust. It reminds you to grow at your own pace and embrace your uniqueness.
